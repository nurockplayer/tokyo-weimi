# Privacy Policy

This site is a static reservation information site. It does not provide an account system and does not ask visitors to submit personal information on the website.

## Data Stored In The Browser

- Age confirmation is stored in `localStorage` so the age gate does not appear on every page view.
- Language preference is stored in `localStorage` so the selected language remains after reload.
- CTA events may be pushed to `window.dataLayer` for future analytics integration. This records interaction names such as phone or LINE button clicks, not form submissions.

## External Links

Phone, LINE, and other external contact links open services outside this site. Those services may apply their own privacy policies.

## Images

Profile images and videos may be displayed from their original public source URLs.

## Contact

For booking details, availability, pricing, and currency exchange questions, confirm directly with customer support by phone or LINE.
